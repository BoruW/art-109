About Me

I am a digital media artist pursuing my MFA in Digital Media Art at San José State University. My practice spans kinetic installations, 3D printing, and creative programming. By combining tools like microcontrollers, CAD modeling, and custom scripts, I build physical and digital environments that explore the intersection of engineered materials and organic movement.

Wish Willow

A kinetic sculpture study exploring how engineered materials can carry an organic, living feeling. Inspired by willow trees swaying in the wind and the cultural tradition of wish trees in northern China, the piece uses a manufactured tree form with an internal joint-and-tendon structure. Chinese calligraphy wish strips hang from the branches to act as leaves. To properly convey a sense of softness and breath, the final installation is designed to be mounted higher than people's head level, allowing the elements to sway naturally above the viewer.

The Train

A real-time generative animation that runs across four synchronized screens using MPI multi-process architecture. Minimalist trains move across the monitors, emitting colorful smoke particles that linger and accumulate over time. Reflecting on China's air-pollution era of the 2000s, the system continuously compares the "pollution" across screens. The most active screen periodically pauses to display rotating Chinese phrases, turning uneven environmental cost into a shared visual spectacle.

[ Image Placeholder: Waves of Love PNG ]

Waves of Love

A computational fabrication project that translates sound waves into a layered, physical object. Using Ableton Live and Blender, specific audio frequencies from a personal recording were isolated to drive the generation of discrete geometric layers. The final piece is fabricated from stacked translucent acrylic. Light passing through the spectrum cuts reveals rhythm and depth, while a planned NFC interaction allows viewers to seamlessly reconnect the static sculpture back to its original temporal audio source.
